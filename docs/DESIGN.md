## UX Principles
* Demo first: every flow must be showable in 30 seconds by a single presenter.
* Simple start: one input, one clear output, progressive detail. Solo scope, no extra screens.
* Trust and clarity: show what Meta AI did and why (labels, confidence).
* Robust live: full live path plus one click cached fallback for stage demos.
* Accessible and fast: readable type, keyboard path, low bandwidth mode.
* Frontier visible: make the hard part obvious (segmentation, reasoning trace, generated visual).
